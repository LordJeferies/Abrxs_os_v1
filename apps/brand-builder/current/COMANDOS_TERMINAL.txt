ABRXOS X · BRAND BUILDER V1

INSTALACIÓN RECOMENDADA
1. Descomprime ABRXOS_X_BRAND_BUILDER_V1.zip.
2. Abre Terminal.
3. Arrastra el archivo INSTALAR_ABRXOS_X_BRAND_BUILDER_V1.command a Terminal y pulsa Enter.

O, si la carpeta está en Descargas con ese nombre:

zsh "$HOME/Downloads/ABRXOS_X_BRAND_BUILDER_V1/INSTALAR_ABRXOS_X_BRAND_BUILDER_V1.command"

EJECUTAR SIN INSTALAR
cd "$HOME/Downloads/ABRXOS_X_BRAND_BUILDER_V1"
python3 app.py

ABRIR HTML SOLO
open "$HOME/Downloads/ABRXOS_X_BRAND_BUILDER_V1/ABRXOS_X_BRAND_BUILDER_V1.html"

LOG
open "$HOME/Library/Logs/abrxos_x_brand_builder_v1.log"

DATOS
open "$HOME/ABRXOS_BRAND_BUILDER_DATA"

EXPORTS
open "$HOME/ABRXOS_BRAND_BUILDER_EXPORTS"
