ABRXOS CONTENT BUILDER V1

INSTALACIÓN RECOMENDADA
1. Descomprime ABRXOS_CONTENT_BUILDER_V1.zip.
2. Abre Terminal.
3. Arrastra INSTALAR_ABRXOS_CONTENT_BUILDER_V1.command a Terminal y pulsa Enter.

O, si está en Descargas:
zsh "$HOME/Downloads/ABRXOS_CONTENT_BUILDER_V1/INSTALAR_ABRXOS_CONTENT_BUILDER_V1.command"

EJECUTAR SIN INSTALAR
cd "$HOME/Downloads/ABRXOS_CONTENT_BUILDER_V1"
python3 app.py

ABRIR HTML SOLO
open "$HOME/Downloads/ABRXOS_CONTENT_BUILDER_V1/ABRXOS_CONTENT_BUILDER_V1.html"

LOG
open "$HOME/Library/Logs/abrxos_content_builder_v1.log"

DATOS
open "$HOME/ABRXOS_CONTENT_BUILDER_DATA"

EXPORTS
open "$HOME/ABRXOS_CONTENT_BUILDER_EXPORTS"
