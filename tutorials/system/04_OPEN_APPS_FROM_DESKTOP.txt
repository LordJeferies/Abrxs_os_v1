OPEN APPS FROM DESKTOP

Finder/Desktop:
- double click `ABRXOS Geometra V3.app`
- double click `ABRXOS Content Builder.app`
- double click `ABRXOS X Brand Builder.app`

Terminal equivalents:
open "$HOME/Desktop/ABRXOS Geometra V3.app"
open "$HOME/Desktop/ABRXOS Content Builder.app"
open "$HOME/Desktop/ABRXOS X Brand Builder.app"

If macOS blocks a locally created app the first time: Control-click/Right-click → Open → Open. Do not reinstall unless the app bundle is actually missing.
